# Artifact scope

This is a curated public source/data snapshot, not a full laboratory backup.
The directory naming preserves the original method dependencies and result
links. R19/R20/R21/R23 core code, graphs, tables, configurations, RTL, inputs,
small logs and reports are retained. Earlier LUT-round method source files
are retained where needed to interpret the development and imports.

Excluded: unrelated research projects, copied third-party author source,
paper PDFs and public-image datasets, vendor executables, compiled caches,
large checkpoints, duplicate archives, and most early-round data. R22 retains
summary results and our driver code, without FABM author/derived RTL.
Positive and negative entries in the included result tables are retained.

`PROVENANCE.json` records each copied member's source hash and the source ZIP
hash. `MANIFEST.json` defines the files distributed here. Historical manifests
may reference local build artifacts outside this public subset. Such paths
are provenance records, not instructions to access another machine.

No extra experiment was performed as part of publication. The title supplied
by the project owner is JARL: Joint Approximate Resynthesis of Dual-Output LUTs
for FPGAs. This repository is a research artifact, not a claim of acceptance.
